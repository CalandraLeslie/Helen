Helen Trethewey

Inclusion Leader · Public Speaker · Dyslexia Specialist

Practical support for parents and teachers of twice exceptional learners.

I am an experienced international educator with over 30 years in the UK, Belgium, Hong Kong, Vietnam and now Sweden. I am a dyslexia specialist and trained assessor. As someone who is dyslexic myself, I understand the frustration of being misunderstood and overlooked at school, and I know how exhausting everyday life can be.
