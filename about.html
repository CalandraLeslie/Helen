About Helen

Inclusion leader, public speaker and dyslexia specialist, based in Stockholm.

I am an experienced international educator with a passion for supporting students and parents of twice exceptional learners. I have worked in education for over 30 years in the UK, Belgium, Hong Kong, Vietnam and now Sweden.

I am a dyslexia specialist and trained assessor. I understand the needs of children and families in the international context and I am able to offer practical advice about resources and services. I have also been a speaker at major educational events in London, Hong Kong and Asia.

As someone who is dyslexic, I live many of the challenges you and your family may be facing. I understand the frustrations of being misunderstood and overlooked at school. I also know how exhausting everyday life can be.

I share advice through webinars and articles, and I offer practical solutions which can be a lifeline.

News and speaking

SENIA Unplugged 2026

Helen presents at this year’s virtual conference in November on two topics: The Emotional Side of Task Initiation, and Promoting student success through developing study skills in Middle School. Both talks give practical strategies for the executive function challenges many students with learning differences face at school.

International Conference on ADHD 2026

In December, Helen presents in person for the first time at the International Conference on ADHD in Baltimore, Maryland, on Task Initiation and Time Blindness.

SENIA Online Conference: The Way Forward

April 2026. Helen recorded her presentation “Don’t Wait and See: Early Intervention strategies to promote belonging and future success”. Many parents and teachers want to wait and see without realising the consequences. Improving foundational skills in the early years is the key to future success.

Autism awareness course on Edu Spark

Helen has created an Autism awareness course for parents and educators who want to better understand the challenges autistic children face. It covers practical strategies, including creating a sensory diet and declarative language.

COBIS Annual Conference, London

Helen spoke at the Annual COBIS conference in London on Innovative Approaches to Meeting the Needs of All Students in the International School, covering practical approaches to early intervention and developing study skills.
