Consultations

Do you need advice or a dyslexia assessment? Ask for advice from experienced international educator Helen Trethewey. Worried that your child is dyslexic or has ADHD? Finding it hard to make sense of all the information online? Want to know how to support your child at home? Get personal, practical advice specific to your child.

Contact Helen

Assessments

Put your mind at rest and organise an assessment with a UK registered specialist assessor. All assessments can be carried out remotely via Zoom. Contact Helen for pricing and availability.

  • Dyslexia screening, ages 5 to 7

    A one-hour, puzzle-based assessment to identify strengths and weaknesses. Brief report and recommendations.

  • Short assessment, ages 8 to 18

    A one-and-a-half-hour assessment of academic and underlying skills. Brief report and recommendations.

  • Full diagnostic dyslexia assessment, ages 6 to 20

    Three hours of standardised testing, with parent and school questionnaires. Diagnostic report with a teaching plan, and recommendations for school and home.

Speaking and workshops

Do you want a guest speaker or staff training? Helen has spoken at conferences around South East Asia, and at events in London and Europe, on topics including working memory difficulties, assistive technology for secondary school pupils and early identification of dyslexia. Her presentations and workshops are informative and practical, with take-aways you can put into practice straight away.

Helen can speak virtually or in person, and has a profile on the Swedish Speakers website.
